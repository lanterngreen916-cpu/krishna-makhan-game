🦚 MAKHAN CHOR 🧈

Little Krishna is inside the Royal Palace to steal delicious makhan! Play the magical flute to put guards to sleep Zzz..., aim & throw stones to break hanging butter pots from palace balconies, and complete all 25 levels!

🎮 EASY CONTROLS (FOR KIDS):
• ← → / A D : Walk Left / Right
• ↑ / W / SPACE : High Jump over guards & platforms
• F / 🪈 FLUTE BTN : Play Flute (Guards sleep Zzz... pass safely!)
• DRAG MOUSE OR TOUCH ON SCREEN : Slingshot Aim & Throw Stones
• LADDER MECHANICS : Jump onto ladder to LATCH & HOLD position! (↑ Climb Up / ↓ Climb Down)
• ⛶ BUTTON : Toggle Fullscreen Mode
